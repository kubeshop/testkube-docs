This inventory is the authoritative manifest for [fully-disconnected installs](/articles/install/air-gapped). Mirror **both** charts below into your internal registry. The **Agent Chart** images are pulled when you create a Runner Agent, after the Control Plane is installed, so a bundle staged from the Control Plane alone will miss them.
