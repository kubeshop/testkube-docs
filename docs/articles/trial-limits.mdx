# Trial Limits & Demo Data

When you start a Testkube trial you get a fully hosted environment, so you can
explore the product without installing anything. To keep it free and instant to
spin up, the trial runs with a few limits that don't apply to a self-hosted or
paid Testkube deployment.

This page explains those limits and the demo data we pre-seed into your trial
environment.

:::info
These limits apply **only to the trial**. When you move to a paid plan and
connect your own infrastructure, they no longer apply - you run with your own
resources and your own quotas.
:::

## Trial duration and limits

The Testkube trial lasts **30 days** from the day you start it. During the trial,
your account is limited to:

| Limit | Trial value |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 30 days |
| Environments | 1 |
| Agents | 1 (fixed) |
| Users | 3 |

When the 30 days are up, the trial is **paused** - but your data stays put.
Your workflows, executions, and results are all still there, ready for when you
pick things back up. To keep going, just [reach out to our team](https://testkube.io/contact)
and we'll help you continue with Testkube.

## The hosted trial runner

Trial environments come with an automatically provisioned, cloud-hosted runner,
so you can run Test Workflows straight away - no agent to install.

:::warning
The hosted trial runner is meant for **evaluation only**. It's a shared,
lightweight environment and is **not sized for production workloads**. For real
testing at scale, connect your own [runner/agent](/articles/agents-overview)
on infrastructure you control.
:::

### Resource limits

The hosted runner is capped at the following resources:

| Resource | Trial limit |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | 2 cores |
| Memory | 2Gi |

Because the runner is on a shared cluster and lightweight, executions may be queued or run
slower during busy periods. That's expected on the trial and doesn't reflect
how Testkube performs on dedicated infrastructure.

## Pre-seeded demo data

So you can see Testkube in action without building anything first, we
**pre-seed your trial environment with demo data**. Example workflows and their
results are already there when you first log in, so the dashboard, execution
history, and insights are populated from the start.

Feel free to explore, edit, re-run, or delete any of it - it's there purely to
give you a realistic starting point for your evaluation.
